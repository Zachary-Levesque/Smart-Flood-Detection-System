# Bill Of Materials

## Required Parts

1. ESP32 development board.
2. Analog water level sensor.
3. Active buzzer module.
4. LED.
5. 220 ohm resistor for the LED.
6. Jumper wires.
7. Breadboard or small perfboard.
8. USB cable.
9. 5 V USB power adapter.
10. Plastic enclosure.

## Optional Parts

1. USB battery backup.
2. Extra mounting tape, screws, or zip ties.

## Notes

1. Use a 3.3 V compatible water sensor.
2. Keep the ESP32 and wiring above the floor.
3. Keep the water sensor at the lowest point where water should be detected.
